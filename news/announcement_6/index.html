I am delighted to share that our paper has been accepted for publication in Information Sciences!

  • Title: Benchmarking the Three-Dimensional and the Numerical Three-Dimensional Matching Problems on the D-Wave Advantage Quantum Annealer[cite: 1]
  • Authors: Komal Pandya and Abyayananda Maiti[cite: 1]
  • Journal: Information Sciences (Impact Factor: 6.0)[cite: 1]

In this work, we present benchmarking and scalability analysis of 3D matching and numerical 3D matching problems mapped onto the D-Wave Advantage quantum annealer, analyzing QUBO formulations and embedding efficiency.
