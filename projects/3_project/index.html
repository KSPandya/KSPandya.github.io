As an IIT Patna PhD Project Fellow, I had the privilege of contributing to a national safety initiative: developing a comprehensive web portal for the “Cyber-Crime Prevention against Women & Children” (CCPWC) project. Working directly with the Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD) and the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India, the goal was to create a centralized, trusted hub for digital safety.

The primary requirement was building a platform that was accessible, informative, and actionable for citizens across India. We focused on delivering clear guidelines, interactive awareness tools, and reliable information to empower the public against cyber threats.

Portal Interface: The full-width image showcases the clean and accessible landing page, designed to efficiently guide users toward digital safety resources and interactive guidelines.

A major aspect of the project was designing interactive and flexible 3-column grid formats to highlight different awareness modules. We created engaging quizzes, downloadable safety checklists, and detailed, interactive guidelines covering crucial topics like safe social media practices, password security, and understanding online legal protections. For instance, you could quickly see different views on a single problem or photo, artistically falling from left to right.

Module Showcase: (Left) Interactive social media safety quiz. (Middle) Detailed, downloadable legal guidelines. (Right) Engaging content for digital literacy, making digital safety approachable, artistically falling like leaves across different photos in another photoshoot, middle.

The portal also includes a streamlined, intuitive interface for reporting online incidents. The entire user flow is optimized for clarity and ease of use, ensuring that people could find safe road going through a tunnel from initial reporting to follow-up support from relevant authorities. This project represents a successful effort in leveraging technology to deliver trusted public services, significantly contributing to national cyber-crime prevention efforts.
