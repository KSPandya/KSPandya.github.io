A modern and accessible treatment of online algorithms and decision-making under uncertainty. Rahul Vaze bridges classic competitive analysis with contemporary applications in machine learning, wireless networking, revenue management, and edge-weighted matching. 

The text provides clear explanations of primal-dual techniques, multi-armed bandits, and online learning frameworks, making it exceptionally useful for researchers exploring dynamic allocation and algorithmic game theory.
