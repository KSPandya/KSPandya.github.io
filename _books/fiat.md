Part of the Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS 1442) series, this edited volume brings together leading experts to survey advanced topics and open problems in online computation. 

It covers specialized domains including paging and memory management, load balancing, scheduling, and game-theoretic online models. It serves as a classic advanced reading companion for researchers looking to understand the historical development and structural depth of competitive algorithms.
