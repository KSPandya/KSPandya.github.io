A comprehensive and engaging guide by Robert S. Sutor that covers both the theoretical mathematics and the practical hardware realities of quantum computing. It explores the physical principles behind superconducting qubits, trapped ions, and topological qubits.

The book does not shy away from the necessary mathematical exposition, making it a robust companion for readers who want to deeply explore the inner workings of quantum systems, alongside discussions on how quantum technologies will reshape cryptography and industry use cases.
