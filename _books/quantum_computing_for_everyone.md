An exceptionally accessible introduction designed for readers with minimal math or physics backgrounds. Chris Bernhardt gently builds up the foundational concepts of qubits, superposition, entanglement, and basic quantum algorithms using linear algebra concepts explained entirely from scratch. 

The book avoids heavy academic jargon while accurately conveying the core principles that make quantum computation fundamentally different from classical computing. It is an ideal starting point for anyone looking to grasp the intuition behind quantum mechanics without getting bogged down in complex multi-particle wave equations.
