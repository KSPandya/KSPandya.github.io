Universally recognized as the foundational bible of online algorithms. Allan Borodin and Ran El-Yaniv provide a rigorous, comprehensive mathematical framework for analyzing decision-making problems where inputs arrive sequentially without future knowledge. 

The book establishes the core concepts of competitive analysis, potential function methods, randomized online algorithms, and adversarial models. It is an indispensable reference text for researchers working in theoretical computer science, online matching, and resource allocation.
