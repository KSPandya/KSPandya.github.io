Had the pleasure of attending the national workshop on **"Engineering and Integration Challenges in Quantum Communication and Quantum Computing"** hosted by **CDAC Pune** from March 21–24, 2024. 

The workshop brought together leading quantum scientists, industry pioneers, national and international leaders, and policy experts to discuss the roadmap, engineering hurdles, and integration challenges in quantum technologies.

Key Highlights:
- **Hands-on Training**: Participated in specialized practical sessions, including hands-on sessions with **Keysight Technologies** focusing on superconducting qubits calibration, measurement setups, and instrumentation.
- **Expert Talks & Keynotes**: Attended insightful talks from various distinguished personalities, including prominent national and international scientists, industry leaders, and policy makers on the future of quantum infrastructure.
- **Quantum Communication & Computing**: Explored advanced topics spanning hardware integration, scalable quantum systems, and secure communication architectures.
