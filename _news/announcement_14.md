Participated and contributed as a volunteer for website design at **Research Scholars Day (RSD 2023)** held at IIT Patna on March 4–5, 2023. 

Additionally, I presented a poster on **"Competitive Analysis of Online Trichromatic Matching"**, showcasing the greedy algorithm for matching in 3-partite, 3-uniform hypergraphs and proving its 1/3-competitiveness using the primal-dual formulation. Also engaged in a meaningful discussion with peers and faculty members on online algorithms.
