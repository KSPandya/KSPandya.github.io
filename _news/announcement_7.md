Successfully delivered the Annual Progress Seminar (APS) at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Patna, presenting research progress on **"Improving Bounds for Edge-Weighted Online Trichromatic Matching"** on August 23, 2024. 

During the seminar, I highlighted our key technical contributions:
* **Continuous Thresholding Mechanism:** Replaced traditional discrete thresholding with a continuous thresholding mechanism to achieve finer granularity in decision-making.
* **Wigner Semicircle Distribution:** Utilized the Wigner semicircle distribution and other principles from Random Matrix Theory (RMT) to dynamically generate thresholds for the matching process and drive the analysis.
* **Improved Competitive Ratio:** Successfully improved upon the previous competitive ratio for the problem.
