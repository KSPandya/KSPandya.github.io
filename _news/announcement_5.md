I had the pleasure of attending the **Quantum India Bengaluru Summit (QIB 2025)** held in Bengaluru. 

The summit brought together leading quantum scientists, industry pioneers, startups, and policy experts to discuss the roadmap for quantum technologies in India.

**Key Highlights:**
* **Quantum Software & Optimization:** Participated in panels on quantum algorithms, NISQ-era optimization (QAOA/QUBO), and quantum simulation frameworks.
* **National Quantum Ecosystem:** Engaged in discussions on scaling quantum research, hardware infrastructure, and industry-academic collaborations.
* **Networking:** Connected with researchers and leaders across quantum hardware, software, and defense-tech applications.
