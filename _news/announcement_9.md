I am delighted to share that our review paper has been accepted for publication in **Physics Reports**!

- **Title**: A Review on Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm and its Variants
- **Authors**: Kostas Blekos*, Dean Brand*, Andrea Ceschini*, Chiao-Hui Chou*, Rui-Hao Li*, **Komal Pandya***, Alessandro Summer* (*equal contribution)
- **Journal**: Physics Reports

In this work, we provide a comprehensive review of the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), exploring its variants, structural properties, performance guarantees, and implementation frameworks in the NISQ era.

* **Comprehensive QAOA Review:** Provided an in-depth overview of the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm, examining its performance across diverse scenarios, applicability to various problem instances, and hardware-specific challenges like noise resilience.
* **Variants & Practical Guidelines:** Conducted a comparative study of selected QAOA extensions and variants, offering a short, practical guide to help address whether the algorithm can outperform classical methods and when it should be utilized.
* **NISQ Implementation Frameworks:** Explored structural properties, performance guarantees, and implementation frameworks for QAOA and its variants specifically tailored for the NISQ era.
