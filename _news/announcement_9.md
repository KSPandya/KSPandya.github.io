I am delighted to share that our review paper has been accepted for publication in **Physics Reports**!

- **Title**: A Review on Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm and its Variants
- **Authors**: Kostas Blekos*, Dean Brand*, Andrea Ceschini*, Chiao-Hui Chou*, Rui-Hao Li*, **Komal Pandya***, Alessandro Summer* (*equal contribution)
- **Journal**: Physics Reports

In this work, we provide a comprehensive review of the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), exploring its variants, structural properties, performance guarantees, and implementation frameworks in the NISQ era.
