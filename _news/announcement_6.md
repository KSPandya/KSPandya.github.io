I am delighted to share that our paper has been accepted for publication in **Information Sciences**!

- **Title:** Benchmarking the Three-Dimensional and the Numerical Three-Dimensional Matching Problems on the D-Wave Advantage Quantum Annealer
- **Authors:** Komal Pandya and Abyayananda Maiti
- **Journal:** *Information Sciences* (Impact Factor: 6.0)

In this work, we present benchmarking and scalability analysis of 3D matching and numerical 3D matching problems mapped onto the D-Wave Advantage quantum annealer, analyzing QUBO formulations and embedding efficiency. 

* **Sparsity-Based 3DM Benchmarking:** Evaluated the computational performance and scalability of the Three-Dimensional Matching (3DM) problem across varying graph sparsities to analyze how edge density affects embedding efficiency and hardware resource usage.
* **QUBO Formulations & Encodings:** Developed and implemented novel Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) formulations for 3DM alongside adapted formulations for Numerical Three-Dimensional Matching (N3DM), utilizing both one-hot and domain-wall constraint encodings.
* **Hardware Topological Metrics:** Benchmarked quantum annealer performance on the D-Wave Advantage system using topological metrics such as physical qubit usage, embedding ratio, average chain length, and minimum Pegasus subgraph requirements.
* **Quantum-Classical Performance Analysis:** Compared quantum annealing results against provably optimal solutions from exact classical solvers (Gurobi and CPLEX) as well as metaheuristic approximations (Simulated Annealing and Tabu Search).
