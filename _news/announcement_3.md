I had the opportunity to attend **DefSAT 2026 (Defence-Space Conference & Expo)** held at the Manekshaw Centre in New Delhi (February 24–26, 2026). 

Themed *"Space at the Core of National Security"*, the conference brought together strategic experts, researchers, and space domain leaders.

**Key Highlights:**
* **Wargaming & Simulations:** Participated in interactive wargaming discussions and strategic scenario planning focused on space domain awareness and satellite defense resilience.
* **Technical Sessions:** Attended panel discussions on orbital security, secure satellite communications, and resilient space architectures.
* **Networking:** Connected with domain experts and industry leaders working on space-tech optimization, satellite constellation networks, and defense technologies.
