I am delighted to share that our paper has been accepted for publication in **IEEE Access**!

- **Title**: Randomized $\epsilon$-RANKING Algorithm for Online Trichromatic Matching
- **Authors**: Komal Pandya and Abyayananda Maiti
- **Journal**: IEEE Access

In this work, we propose and analyze a randomized $\epsilon$-ranking algorithm to address the challenges of online trichromatic matching, establishing performance guarantees and competitive ratios.
