I am delighted to share that our paper has been accepted for publication in **IEEE Access**!

- **Title**: Randomized $\epsilon$-RANKING Algorithm for Online Trichromatic Matching
- **Authors**: Komal Pandya and Abyayananda Maiti
- **Journal**: IEEE Access

### Overview & Core Contributions

In this work, we extend the fundamental idea of RANKING from classical online bipartite matching (OBM) to model and solve the complex combinatorial challenges inherent in **online trichromatic matching (OTM)**. 

* **Bridging OBM and Multi-Partition Allocation**: While classical OBM governs dynamic allocation between two vertex sets under uncertainty, multi-sided systems frequently require multi-partition interactions. We formalize the structural constraints and competitive dynamics of matching across three distinct classes.
* **Partition Synchronization Dynamics**: We address the intricate synchronization challenges that arise in multi-sided environments where one intermediate partition is fixed (offline) while the other two partitions arrive dynamically online.
* **Randomized $\epsilon$-Ranking Framework**: We propose and analyze a novel randomized $\epsilon$-ranking algorithm, establishing rigorous performance guarantees, competitive ratios, and analytical bounds for multi-partite dynamic allocation.
