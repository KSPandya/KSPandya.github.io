Successfully delivered the Annual Progress Seminar (APS) at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Patna, presenting research progress on “Primes Based Randomization for Online Trichromatic Matching” on August 30, 2023.

In this seminar, I demonstrated how deep number-theoretic and analytic principles: specifically **Euler's theorem**, the **Prime Number Theorem (PNT)**, the **Riemann zeta function**, **modular arithmetic structures**, and **distribution properties of primes**, can be harnessed for advanced randomization. I showed how these mathematical tools enable the design and rigorous probabilistic analysis of randomized algorithms for solving online trichromatic matching and dynamic allocation problems in multi-partition hypergraphs.
